# The rack as a real one

Status: accepted · 2026-11-22

## Context
The rack was a zoomable picture of a rack.

## Decision
- Dragging a device in its rack, a neighbour trades places with it only once it is carried past
  that neighbour's middle (2026-11-27: the others jumped as soon as it was touched).
  Carried, it is lifted over the others: its shadow falls on them (every other shadow lies under
  every face).
- Rails at the window's sides, faces screwed into them (no ears), units numbered from the top.
  On the desk a face has no screws (2026-11-27: nothing to screw it to, and they sat over its
  name).
- The rack is always at 100%: the wheel scrolls, there is no zoom.
- One light over the whole rack, falling from the top left: the paint as it is in the corner
  nearer it, falling into shade towards the far one (shade only: a light added looked milky), evenly
  across every device and only on them (not on the rack's gaps or the desk), fixed to the rack; no face has a light of its own there (2026-11-27: each
  face's sheen, light at its top and dark at its bottom, made the light go in waves down a stack).
  The same over MIXER's modules and over the devices on the desk: one style everywhere.
- Faces are as wide as the rack, with vents in the room left: a fine honeycomb of small
  hexagons laid on the face's own grid, whole holes only, the field as high as the frames (its first and last rows touching their top and bottom), so where the free space runs from one
  unit into the next the holes run on and a 3U device reads as one (2026-11-27, variant 1 of ten
  in `docs/design/vents/`: the rows of slots, a block in each unit, made it look like three 1U
  devices). Frames that don't fit go on to
  more units (variant E): the lower units fill first across the whole width, the unit beside
  the name takes what is left, and every unit's frames start at the left.
- A line is printed down each side of a rack face, from its top screw to its bottom one, a
  little room round their heads (2026-11-27: the sides of a border round the face; the whole
  border, broken round everything on it, was too busy and waits in the ROADMAP).
- Racks stand side by side only when the devices don't fit the window's height.
