# The rack as a real one

Status: accepted · 2026-11-22

## Context
The rack was a zoomable picture of a rack.

## Decision
- Rails at the window's sides, faces screwed into them (no ears), units numbered from the top.
- The rack is always at 100%: the wheel scrolls, there is no zoom.
- Faces are as wide as the rack, with vents in the room left. Frames that don't fit go on to
  more units (variant E): the lower units fill first across the whole width, the unit beside
  the name takes what is left, and every unit's frames start at the left.
- Racks stand side by side only when the devices don't fit the window's height.
