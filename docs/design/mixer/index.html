Channels make sound, groups mix some of them and pass the mix on (Easy Effects), masters at the right mix it all and play it; the outlined one is MAIN, where new apps play. MIX TO / RECORD TO: lit = linked; click to link or unlink (a key that would feed a strip back into itself refuses). Fader: drag or wheel; double-click: 100%. BAL: drag up or down; double-click: centre. CUE: hear a strip in the MAIN output, nothing else changes (several mix). MAIN IN: where new apps record from. PROFILE / PORT: the card's. #id: opens ENGINE with that node. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z, presets on the LCD. The strip's last line on the screen is where its sound ends up, through effects too.
