HOW TO READ IT

Channels (left): what makes sound. Buses (right): where sound goes: outputs, and apps that record.

An assign key lit = that channel is linked to that bus. It is on or off: in PipeWire a link has no level.

Faders: a channel's is its stream's volume; a bus's is its device's volume. Drag, or the wheel; double-click: 0 dB.

CUE: listen to a channel alone, in your headphones, without changing anything.

MAIN: where new apps play.

HEARD ON: where the sound really ends up, through effects too.

Hover anything to see what it is in PipeWire.
