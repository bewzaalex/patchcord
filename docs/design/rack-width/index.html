THE RACK'S WIDTH

A wide window. While every device fits one unit in a rack narrower than the window, the rack is only as wide as the widest device needs (in one unit), and stands in the middle of the window; the desk shows at both sides. Once a device would need a second unit in that width, or the window is narrower, it is as now: the rack fills the window.
